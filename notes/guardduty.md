# GuardDuty Notes

GuardDuty is an AWS-managed threat detection service. A local emulator may not reproduce its detection models or managed data sources.

For each hypothetical detection, document:
- Threat hypothesis
- Required telemetry and prerequisites
- Expected finding or signal
- Severity and response target
- Validation method
- Known blind spots
- Tuning/suppression strategy
- Escalation and containment options

Practice with synthetic findings and tabletop exercises unless an approved AWS sandbox is available.
